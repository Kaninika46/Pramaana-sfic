Organise evidence of flood damage to your crop

Pramaana helps farmers and field helpers in the Ghatal area record suspected flood damage, keep photographs and documents safe with a digital fingerprint, and prepare a clear evidence file (dossier) for a human reviewer. বন্যায় ফসলের ক্ষতির প্রমাণ গুছিয়ে রাখুন।

Important: Pramaana does not file an insurance claim or inform your insurer. If your crop is insured, inform the insurer or official channel yourself as soon as possible.

What happens to a report

  1. You report the suspected loss and give consent.
  2. Photos and documents are stored unchanged, and a SHA-256 fingerprint is recorded for each.
  3. The field location and how it was recorded (phone GPS or typed) are saved.
  4. A reviewer runs satellite analysis — only if Earth Engine is genuinely connected.
  5. The reviewer re-checks every file's fingerprint and prepares a labelled dossier.
  6. The reviewer records a decision. You see the status and any notes on the tracking page.
