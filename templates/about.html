About Pramaana and its limits

Read this before relying on anything Pramaana produces.

What Pramaana is — and is not

  • An evidence-organisation and claim-preparation tool built as a student public-interest technology project.
  • Not a court, government authority, insurer, legal-aid authority or decision-maker.
  • Not connected to any DLSA, insurer, government department or official portal. "Reviewer" means a signed-in member of the project's internal review workflow.
  • It never files an intimation or claim, and never promises approval, compensation or admissibility.
  • Intended deployment region: Ghatal subdivision, Paschim Medinipur. Each case uses only its own recorded location.

  • Sentinel-2 NDVI (COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED, SCL cloud mask): median NDVI {{ params.s2_pre_window_days }} days before vs {{ params.s2_post_window_days }} days after, with the cloud-free fraction reported.
